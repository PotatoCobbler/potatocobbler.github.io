Thermal polarization in NMR

This interactive NMR polarization calculator shows the thermal equilibrium polarization of nuclear spins and electrons as a function of magnetic field and temperature. The graph plots polarization against the ratio of magnetic field to temperature, \(B/T\), allowing different nuclei to be compared directly through their gyromagnetic ratios.

For a spin-\(\tfrac{1}{2}\) particle at thermal equilibrium, the magnitude of the polarization is given by the Boltzmann expression

\[ P = \tanh\left( \frac{\hbar |\gamma| B} {2 k_{\mathrm B} T} \right) \]

where \(\hbar\) is the reduced Planck constant, \(\gamma\) is the gyromagnetic ratio, \(B\) is the magnetic field, \(k_{\mathrm B}\) is the Boltzmann constant, and \(T\) is the absolute temperature.

For nuclei with spin \(I > \tfrac{1}{2}\), the polarization is calculated from the complete Boltzmann distribution over the \(2I+1\) Zeeman energy levels.
