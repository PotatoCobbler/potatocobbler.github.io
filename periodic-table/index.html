Periodic table

Periodic table of NMR isotopes

Most periodic tables have stuff on them we do not really care about: atomic weight, electron shells and, if you're lucky, ionisation potentials. Useless! So, I made a periodic table that includes stuff NMR spectroscopists actually care about: NMR active isotopes, resonance frequencies, spin, natural abundance, and receptivity. The poster also includes a classical derivation of the origin of spin, the gyromagnetic ratio, and the Larmor frequency. For the most commonly used constants, their value is provided in different, relevant units. A0 format (841 x 1189 mm) on canvas, ideal for the office, lab or simply at home. Available upon request.
